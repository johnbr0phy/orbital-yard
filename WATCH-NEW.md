# Watching and flying Tribute New

The battle opens in director view. The camera dock offers Action, All ships, Top down, Hero, Follow, Crew and Take control. Action reframes recent combat; wide views include every hull. Drag or keyboard movement returns to free camera. The details panel is optional: closing it preserves following, and Crew reopens it.

Each ship has a persistent crew identity derived from its seed and race. The feature-by-feature approach was informed by the local /faces project; this is a separate compact Canvas renderer, not an import of that project's illustration engine. All 18 fleets have original crew interpretations with varying skull proportions, eyes, hair, markings, uniforms and bridge arrangements. Portraits draw only on selection. These are stylized fictional studies, not exact screen-character depictions.

Select an arrived ship, then Take control. W/S changes throttle; A/D turns; up/down pitches. Space requests its native weapon against a forward target, retaining targeting limits. Escape returns the helm to AI and keeps following. Touch controls are included. Damage still reduces available speed. This first helm mode uses a forward first-person camera with a light bridge overlay, not a walkable 3D cockpit.

Validation: deterministic crew diversity across every fleet, independent panel closing/follow state, camera presets, player control through real simulation ticks, weapon and damage regressions, and a bounded 26-ship combat exchange. Layout and crew styles inspected in isolated Canvas/HTML previews; no large-fleet GPU stress run.
