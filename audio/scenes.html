Tribute War: the scene set

Ten scripted moments from seeded wars, each with its picture and its sound. Before is the build that shipped with pull requests 7 and 8; After is the object-based mix. For the two fly-bys, Ship alone is the same capture with only the passing ship audible. Headphones recommended (the nearest few sounds are rendered with HRTF). Space plays and pauses, B and A switch.
