# Minbari fleet rebuild

The previous ordinary builder returned only Nial fighters. The forge tried to fill escort and capital bands with that builder and ultimately substituted fighters. Every crown also built the same Sharlin.

The ordinary roster now covers Nial, a smaller Tishat interpretation, Torotha assault frigate, Tinashi war frigate, Tigara attack cruiser and Morshin carrier. The crown draws Sharlin, Tigara and Morshin; the largest slot remains Sharlin. The muster targets 46% fighters, 36% escorts and 18% line ships before its three crown slots and hero. Minbari escorts have a race-specific size band, so frigates actually reach escort stations. Other fleets keep their existing thresholds.

Sharlin was reconstructed from the model and orthographic references below: tall dorsal sail, two lower sails in a narrow V, backward tail points, curved flank lips and ribbed gills, with a forward neutron-cannon pair. Tinashi is a longer, slimmer forked hull with smaller aft sails; Torotha has a broad flat forward shell, pinched waist and smaller aft shell. Morshin has a low carrier shell over separated hangar cheeks. The extra classes are original interpretations of the screen/licensed model vocabulary, not imported or exact replicas of third-party assets. Game lengths for the intermediate classes are chosen to keep the fleet's scale hierarchy readable, not presented as definitive canon measurements.

Six sister configurations reshape the connected shell and sails rather than adding generic blisters: standard, long-range, carrier, survey, armored and support. Existing seeded finish varies their blue/violet surface. The model study includes all seven classes and now fits complete ships on narrow screens.

## Research references

- [Canison Sharlin 3D model](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3784126) — silhouette and fin/body proportions visually inspected.
- [Mark Kane Sharlin orthographic drawing](https://rpggamer.org/uploaded_images/warcruiser_sharlin.jpg) — front/side/top views inspected for the lower-fin V, gills and aft taper. Its hosting page's numerical stats were not used.
- [Tinashi rendered reference](https://kitsunesden.xyz/SF-Conversions/Rifts-B5-Ships/Minbari_Tinashi_War_Frigate.jpg) — long ribbed hull and smaller aft sails visually inspected; fan RPG statistics were not used.
- [Tyrel Lohr Tinashi model](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/minbari-tinashi-war-frigate) — model description and escort role.
- [Torotha model reference](https://www.cgtrader.com/free-3d-models/space/spaceship/minbari-torotha-assault-frigate) — preview inspected for broad head, narrow waist and aft shell; no asset downloaded or redistributed.
- [Morshin model listing](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/minbari-morshin) — carrier class/model reference.
- [Agents of Gaming catalogue](https://www.agentsofgaming.com/shopping.htm) — licensed expanded fleet roster rather than treating every design as an on-screen class.

## Validation

Seven class meshes, deterministic generation, six connected sister configurations, actual worker band assignment and a 48-per-side muster were tested. The muster contains real frigates, fewer than 60% fighters and multiple non-fighter classes. Mesh checks remain below 4,000 triangles at battle test quality; the higher-quality viewer Sharlin is approximately 4,300. Mount seating, scaling, uniqueness, weapon, rendering, effects, damage, watch and star-system regression checks passed. Clay sheets and an isolated WebGL Sharlin were visually inspected. No large-battle GPU benchmark was run.
